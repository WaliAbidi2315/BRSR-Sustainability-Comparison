Cross-company comparison

Reviewing sustainability indicators across financial companies.

A workbook-based comparison of selected environmental intensity and workforce measures across four companies. This portfolio project uses only the designated cross-company comparison worksheet; other sheets were excluded as instructed.

Project objective

Compare selected sustainability disclosures with emphasis on environmental intensity, resource use and workforce indicators. The analysis is descriptive and does not claim to measure each company's overall sustainability performance.

Environmental indicators

GHG emissions and water intensity relative to revenue, including per-employee measures.

Workforce indicators

Employee scale, turnover and the female wage-share proxy as listed in the worksheet.

Consistent display

Side-by-side metrics with reporting-period labels and missing values preserved.

Source scope

One selected cross-company worksheet is used; the other two sheets are intentionally excluded.

Limitations

Reporting periods are not identical and definitions may vary across disclosures. Some worksheet cells are blank. The female wage-share proxy should not be treated as a gender pay-gap or equal-pay measure. Validate source figures against company reports before drawing conclusions.
